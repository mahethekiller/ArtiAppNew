**Aarti Sangrah (आरती संग्रह)** is your ultimate digital companion for daily Hindu prayers, spiritual rituals, and temple contemplation. Immerse yourself in devotion with a curated collection of **61 authentic Aartis** spanning **13 major Hindu deities**, complete with interactive virtual Pooja elements that bring the sacred temple experience directly to your mobile device.

Whether you are performing daily home Pooja, celebrating festivals like Navratri and Diwali, or seeking momentary peace, Aarti Sangrah provides a deeply immersive and visually stunning spiritual experience.

Unlike simple text apps, Aarti Sangrah features a sacred WebGL interactive engine:
- 🌸 **Pushp Arpan (Offer Flowers):** Shower beautiful virtual Marigold and Rose petals over your favorite deities.
- 🔔 **Temple Bell (Ghanti):** Ring the sacred brass temple bell with realistic resonance and haptic feedback.
- 🐚 **Shankh (Conch Shell):** Play the deeply resonant Shankh sound to purify your surroundings.
- 🪔 **Virtual Diya:** Experience the warmth of a gently burning Diya flame.

### 📖 Premium Reading Experience
- **Dual Script (Hindi & Hinglish):** Read Aartis in pure Devanagari Hindi or easy-to-read English Transliteration (Hinglish).
- **Adjustable Text Size:** Easily increase or decrease font size for comfortable reading.
- **Three Sacred Themes:** Choose between Ivory Day, vintage Parchment, or Temple Night (Dark Mode) to suit your environment.

### 🙏 Comprehensive Aarti Collection (60+ Aartis)
Find complete, authentic lyrics for:
- **Maa Durga & Navratri:** All 9 forms (Shailputri, Brahmacharini, Chandraghanta, etc.), Ambe Gauri.
- **Shri Ganesh:** Jai Mangal Murti, Sukh Karta Dukh Harta, Ganesh Chalisa.
- **Shri Hanuman:** Aarti Kije Hanuman Lala Ki, Hanuman Chalisa.
- **Maa Lakshmi & Vishnu:** Om Jai Jagdish Hare, Diwali Mahalakshmi, Satyanarayan Aarti.
- **Lord Shiva:** Om Jai Shiv Omkara, Har Har Mahadev.
- **Other Deities:** Khatu Shyam Baba, Ganga Maiya, Shri Krishna (Janmashtami Special), Surya Dev, Saraswati Mata, and Special Vrat Aartis (Chhath, Karwa Chauth).

- **Favorites & History:** Save your daily Aartis to a dedicated Favorites tab for instant access.
